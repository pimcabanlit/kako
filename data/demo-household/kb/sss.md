# SSS contributions

Plain-language reference for the Villanueva household (synthetic). Kako reads it to explain a plan;
it never uses it to calculate anything.

- The Social Security System (SSS) is the social insurance program for private-sector workers.
- Employee and employer both contribute every month. The employee's share is deducted from the payslip.
- Members can claim benefits for sickness, maternity, disability, retirement and death, and can apply
  for salary loans.
- Kako reads take-home pay after these deductions (`income.monthly_net`). It never computes the
  contributions; the payslip is the source.
