# Emergency fund

Kako's planning rule for the Villanueva household (synthetic).

- The emergency fund is the one account with `account.purpose` = `emergency`.
- The household's target is a number of months of essential expenses (`household.emergency_months_target`).
  The Villanuevas chose 3 months.
- The runway is the money in liquid accounts divided by essential monthly expenses. Long-term savings
  that are locked up don't count.
- Months to the target: the gap between the target and the liquid balance, divided by what goes into the
  fund each month, rounded up.
