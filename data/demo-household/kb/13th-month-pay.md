# 13th-month pay

Plain-language reference for the Villanueva household (synthetic). Kako reads it to explain a plan;
it never uses it to calculate anything, and it is not legal or tax advice.

- In the Philippines, rank-and-file employees in the private sector who worked at least a month in the
  calendar year receive 13th-month pay: at least one twelfth of the basic salary they earned that year.
- It is paid by December 24, so it arrives once a year, in December.
- Part of it can be free of income tax. Kako never calculates tax; the payslip shows what was withheld.
- Kako records the amount the household expects as a fact (`income.thirteenth_month`) and shows it
  separately in December. It is not part of monthly income, so a plan never depends on it month to month.
