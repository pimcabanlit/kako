# Pag-IBIG Fund and MP2 savings

Plain-language reference for the Villanueva household (synthetic). Kako reads it to explain a plan;
it never uses it to calculate anything, and it never recommends an investment product.

- The Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) is a savings and housing loan fund. Employees contribute monthly through
  payroll, and their employers add a share. Kako reads take-home pay after the deduction.
- Members can apply for housing loans, which matters for a household saving for a house.
- MP2 is the fund's voluntary savings program. Money put in is generally held for five years, so Kako
  doesn't count an MP2-style account as liquid (`account.liquid` = `no`) when it works out the
  emergency-fund runway.
