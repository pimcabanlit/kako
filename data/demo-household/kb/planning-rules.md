# The household's planning rules

How Kako plans for the Villanueva household (synthetic). The engine computes every figure; agents
explain and propose; the household decides.

1. Every peso of the monthly surplus is allocated: extra on a debt, into the emergency fund, or to a
   goal. Nothing allocated may exceed the surplus.
2. Debts are paid in the household's strategy order. The Villanuevas chose avalanche: the highest
   interest rate first. When a debt is paid off, its whole payment moves to the next debt.
3. Minimum payments are always paid.
4. A change to the plan is a proposal. It takes effect only when an adult approves it.
5. When life changes (a new job, a new statement), the old value is replaced, not overwritten, so the
   household can always see what a plan was based on.
